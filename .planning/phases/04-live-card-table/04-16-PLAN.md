<objective>
The card HUD's root and its status strip: the `ScreenGui`, the scale frame, the zone frames, and the
one line of text that carries every hint, countdown, strike warning and cue.

Purpose: UX-03's constant and UX-01's geometry become real pixels here, and this is the first plan in
the phase whose output a machine cannot judge. **Everything decided is already decided** — the
geometry is plan 04-08's `Layout.solve`, the words are plan 04-11's `Hints`, the durations and caps
are plan 04-01's `Config.Card`. So this plan's discipline is the deliverable: the renderer
**applies** results and computes nothing. As the research puts it, every place this phase is tempted
to add logic to the Roblox-touching layer is a place a test can no longer reach it.

Two decisions land here concretely:

- **UX-03 / V-24, verified:** `ScreenInsets = Enum.ScreenInsets.CoreUISafeInsets`, set **explicitly**
  and never left to the default. The documentation recommends exactly this member "for any
  `ScreenGui` that contains interactive and/or important UI elements such as buttons and status
  messages", which is what the hand, the tappable left pile and the AFK countdown are.
- **D-36 and checker FLAG F4:** a `UITextSizeConstraint` on the strip label, because
  `GuiService.PreferredTextSize` scales text **even when `TextScaled = false`**. The accepted cost is
  stated plainly: a player who chose **Largest** text gets smaller text here than they asked for, in
  the exact place strike warnings appear. That was chosen over wrapping (which forces a third
  re-derivation of the HandZone budget) and over truncation (which would cut the clause saying what
  is about to happen).

**D-34 binds every line:** vanilla Roblox UI only. `ScreenGui`, `Frame`, `ImageLabel`, `TextLabel`,
`TextButton`, `UIScale`, `UICorner`, `UIStroke`, `UIGradient`, `UIListLayout` and plain Luau. No
Fusion, no React-lua, no `wally`, and `rokit install` cannot work in this container at all. **If an
executor believes a library is required, that is a BLOCKER to raise with the owner, never something
to install.**

Output: `CardTheme.luau`, `CardHud.luau`, `CardStrip.luau`, and the renderer-discipline gate.
</objective>
