<objective>
Close the phase honestly: measure the suite against its budget, prove every mutation gate has been
watched fail, write down exactly which requirements a machine closed and which a human must, and
hand the next phases what they must not rediscover.

Purpose, and the reason this plan is not a formality. Two facts about this project:

1. **~31 assertions structurally incapable of firing** were found across Phases 2 and 3, *by people
   actively looking for them*. The mutation ledger in task 2 is the only thing that makes "the gate
   is green" mean "the gate can go red".
2. **Four of this phase's five ROADMAP success criteria are device-dependent** and this container has
   neither Studio nor a phone. Criterion 1 (tap-select/tap-pass) and criterion 4 (auto-pass and
   bench) are mostly machine-checkable; criteria 2 (immediacy at 150-300 ms), 3 (phone landscape,
   48 px on glass, safe areas, hints) and 5 (starting gun, colour-blind legibility) are not. This
   plan writes that split down **before** anyone reads a green suite as a closed phase.

CI has already overshot its original ~20 s target twice with owner approval — 15.94 s at Phase 1,
~25-26 s at Phase 2, 29.9 s at Phase 3. **A third overshoot is an explicit owner decision, not
something to absorb**, which is why task 1 ends in a decision checkpoint if the budget is missed.

Output: a completed `04-VALIDATION.md`, a deferred-items file, updated debt rows, updated STATE, and
a human checkpoint that says plainly what is still open.
</objective>
