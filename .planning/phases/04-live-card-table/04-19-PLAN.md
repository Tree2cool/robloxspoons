<objective>
The table as a place: travelling cards, other seats' stacks, the who-went callout, the flourish
surface and the audio.

Purpose, and why none of this is decoration:

- **D-19:** during the start-up ramp cards are **visibly travelling the ring toward you**, so the
  wait reads as the deal arriving rather than as nothing happening. The ramp is real and measured —
  **~8.3 s at N = 10** against a 4.6 s median time-to-first-completion at N = 3. It is diegetic,
  needs no text, and it doubles as the thing that teaches a new player how the chain flows.
- **D-27:** the table must stay a place, not a wallpaper. You watch stacks grow, see cards travel,
  and see someone stand up and run. Making it a backdrop was rejected **explicitly**, because that
  turns the card phase back into the menu transition the Core Value forbids.
- **D-31:** the table sees the diegetic event **plus** a brief callout naming who went. The
  information is already public and *"someone is already out there"* is the pressure the whole design
  runs on — there are two fewer spoons than players, and a player head-down in their hand on a phone
  would otherwise miss it.
- **D-33:** a completion sting plus quiet feedback for **your own** draw and pass, and deliberately
  little or nothing for other seats' passes, because nine players passing constantly is a cacophony.
  **A starting gun that makes no sound is not a starting gun.**
- **CARD-13:** other seats get **stack height only**. No numerals, no faces. Pile-depth precision is
  asymmetric on purpose: an exact count on your own two piles, because your left-pile depth is the
  most actionable number you have, and nine floating numbers on a phone is clutter.

Output: `CardTableView.luau`, `CardCallout.luau`, `CardFlourish.luau`, `CardAudio.luau`, and the
surface gate.
</objective>

| Concern | Rule |
|---|---|
| stack height | `SEAT_STACK_STUDS_PER_CARD * math.min(count, SEAT_STACK_CAP)`, **strictly monotone** in the inbox count. One extra card must be a visible step at the reference viewport; the real tuning is on the real map (**M-12**) |
| numerals | **none, ever, over another seat** (CARD-13). Exact counts exist only on the local player's own two panels |
| faces | a travelling card and another seat's stack are **always face-down**. A public `Passed` carries a `cardId` and no rank, so there is nothing to render face-up and nothing may be inferred |
| hops | `TWEEN.HOP` along a shallow arc, peak one stud; **cap `TABLE_HOP_CONCURRENT_CAP` concurrent**. Beyond the cap, skip the hop and just update the stack height — a dropped cosmetic is better than a frame-rate collapse during the ramp |
| the dealer | **no marker anywhere.** The dealer's left pile *is* the remaining deck, so their stack is the tallest object on the table by construction. The role is true by geometry rather than by a flag someone has to keep in sync |
| seat poses | from `RoundStart`. The map's `Seats` folder lives under the map's **`Data`**, which is never cloned into Workspace, so the client cannot read a seat pose from the replicated tree and the server publishes it narrowly (plan 04-12's recorded decision) |

**`defaults()`** returns the two `"Plain"` entries executing `Flourish.DEFAULTS`' keyframe data:

four cards from the left-pile corner into the fan, staggered 0.1 s, settled by 0.6 s.

**A registered flourish receives only `ctx`** — `root`, `kind`, `seat`, `isLocalSeat`,
