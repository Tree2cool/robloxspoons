<objective>
The phase's evidence, made runnable, and the phase's cost, measured once and correctly.

**Three probes, because three of this phase's claims can only be settled on hardware, and a check
nobody can run at 1am is a check that does not happen.** Each is a committed script with its own
non-vacuity built in, because this repo's recurring failure is not a missing check — it is a check
that cannot fail:

- **M-14's greyscale grid** must **compute Rec. 709 luma itself** and must **never** drive
  desaturation with `ColorCorrectionEffect`. This phase's own DEBT-RECHECK records that a
  `ViewportFrame` renders no post-processing and that the effect cannot be relied on for card UI, and
  whether it reaches an `AlwaysOnTop` SurfaceGui is unknown — it bypasses the normal 3D path by
  design. **A probe that silently fails to desaturate shows the reviewer the COLOURED faces, and they
  pass easily.** That is vacuous in exactly the direction that hides the defect the check exists to
  find. So the probe computes luma, and carries a `SUIT_RED` control swatch that **must read
  mid-grey** — if the swatch is still red the run is void regardless of what the faces looked like.
- **M-20's privacy probe** must scan `Players/*/PlayerGui` as well as `Workspace` and
  `ReplicatedStorage`, from a server script **and** from a second real client. U2 is a claim about
  client-created `PlayerGui` descendants, and the first draft's scan never visited `PlayerGui` at all.
  **Step 1 of the procedure is to plant a `CardSlot` Part with a rank attribute under `workspace` and
  watch the probe fire.** A privacy gate that has never been seen to fail is not evidence, and D-45 is
  the largest new risk in the phase.
- **M-19's projection probe** ties the pure model to the engine: the 8 corners of every card through
  the real `Camera:WorldToViewportPoint` within 1 px at three viewports, then a 2 px debug overlay on
  a **notched** phone where the pure quad, the 3D card and the hit button must coincide. A zero-inset
  device does not satisfy it.

**And the budget, measured the way Phase 4 learned to measure it.** Phase 4 cost **+0.99 s** across
**+214 cases**. Its own log records the lesson in one line: *take ONE controlled pre/post measurement
across the whole phase; use per-wave deltas only to attribute cost, never to total it.* Summing the
per-wave deltas over-reported Phase 4 by more than 2x (~2.20 s against a true +0.99 s), because each
delta carries about +/-0.5 s of container noise and several waves measured negative, which is not
physically possible as a real cost.

Output: three probes, a gate on the probes, the manual-check rows pointing at them, and one budget
figure.
</objective>
