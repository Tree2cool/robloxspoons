<objective>
The hand itself: 3D cards the player can see, 2D buttons the player taps, and motion that decorates
the model instead of gating it.

**The hit model does not change at all, and that is the point (D-47).** Visual is 3D, input is 2D.
One invisible `TextButton` per slot, positioned on the projected rect, in the existing
`CoreUISafeInsets` gui. Cards and their SurfaceGuis are never `Active`. This keeps Phase 4's hit
model, its 48 px assertion and its `layout.spec` discipline intact, and it is **why this phase is
viable where D-26 was not**. Raycasting against card geometry was considered and rejected: it makes
the target size unassertable again, which is the thing being fixed. A later reader must not re-open
that.

**Motion never gates input (D-50).** CARD-14's prediction is unchanged: the local model updates
immediately and the tween is decoration over it. **The card leaves the hand model in the same frame
the intent is sent.** A pass animation that had to finish before the next tap would destroy the
tap-tap-tap scramble the game is built on, and would silently lengthen every pass by its own duration
in a game scored in deciseconds. Hit rects **jump** to the final layout at the instant of a commit
while the visuals tween — Phase 4's accepted FLAG F7, carried verbatim — and rects are computed from
the **final pose**, never from a tween's current CFrame.

**One duration for travel (D-51).** `TWEEN.CARD_MOVE = 0.18` drives draw, pass, return and deal.
`SELECT`, `FLIP` and `CLOSE_GAP` are not travel and are unchanged. A per-path duration is how a
presentation change becomes a pacing change nobody intended.

**Two accepted residuals, implemented as written and not improved.** (1) The lifted card's visible
overhang on its right neighbour belongs to the neighbour's rect, so tapping it selects the neighbour —
widening the lifted rect would shrink the neighbour below the floor. (2) Hit rects snap at commit
while visuals tween, so the two disagree for up to `CLOSE_GAP`; the alternative is a tap landing on a
slot that is about to move, and D-02 forbids adding a delay to resolve it.

This plan also **deletes `CardFace`'s retired 2D card API**, because it is now dead: this is its last
caller.

**And it updates its own call site.** `CardHand.new`'s signature changes here and `CardClient` is its
only caller. An earlier draft left that update to plan 09, which would have left the tree failing
`verify.luau`'s typecheck for a whole wave. **The party that changes a signature fixes its callers**,
so the one `CardHand.new(...)` call and its `handCF` supplier are in this plan. Everything else in
`CardClient` — the rail, the relayout set, the deal geometry, the headers — stays plan 09's.

Output: the rewritten hand, the deleted dead code, and the motion gate.
</objective>

**The motion table, as shipped. Every duration from `Config.Card.TWEEN`; no literal.**

| Motion | Path | Easing | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select / deselect | slot pose <-> slot pose with `lifted = true` (the lift is along the card's own up vector, inside `Projection.poses`) | Quad Out | `SELECT` 0.08 |
| Draw (predicted) | left-pile anchor, face-down, -> slot pose, shallow arc peaking 0.5 studs toward the camera | Quad Out | `CARD_MOVE` |
| Flip | on the server's answer carrying `cardId` and face: width 1 -> 0 -> 1 | — | `FLIP` 0.12 |
| Pass | slot pose -> right-pile anchor, scaling to 60% | Quad In | `CARD_MOVE` |
| Return (D-11) | right-pile anchor -> original slot, **or** slot -> left pile for a rolled-back draw | **Back Out** (slight overshoot) | `CARD_MOVE` |
| Forced out (`ValveForcedPass`) | identical to Pass, plus a **neutral** ring on the face stroke | Quad In | `CARD_MOVE` + `FORCED_RING` 0.4 |
| Close the gap | neighbours slide to the re-centred fan | Quad Out | `CLOSE_GAP` 0.12 |
| Deal | left-pile anchor -> each slot, staggered 0.1 s | Quad Out | `CARD_MOVE` |

A return reads as a *return* by **overshoot easing, not by being slower** — that is the whole reason
one duration is tolerable (Open Question 4; the fallback, if it reads wrong on a device, is one
`RETURN_MOVE` constant and a recorded D-51 exception).

**Pile flight endpoints.** The 2D panel centre is **unprojected** onto the hand plane with
`Projection.unproject`, so a card flies to where the panel is actually drawn. No new tappable object
is created, and `CardPiles` is untouched.

Structure:

- **Created once.** The five hit buttons and the backdrop are built in `new` and never recreated;
  `apply` shows, hides and moves them. A tap can therefore never land on a button mid-destruction.

- **Carriers are pooled, not recreated.** At most six exist (five hand cards plus one arriving or
  returning), which is D-54's budget. A carrier is **mutated** between visual states and never
  destroyed and rebuilt, because a destroyed instance mid-tween is how a renderer drops a card on the
  floor.
- **No `Layout.Slot`, `solution.slots` or `solution.fan` is read by the new file.** The shim exists
  for `CardClient` alone until plan 09; a new reader here would hold the deprecation open.
- **Poses come from `Projection.poses(handSize, liftedSlot)`** and are applied as
  `carrier:setPose(pose, handCF())`. The hand computes no studs, no degrees, no radius and no arc.
- **Hit buttons come from `Projection.slots(viewport, handSize, liftedSlot)`** and are positioned
  with `hud:pxRect(...)`. **Never assign a projected rect directly** — the `Root`'s `UIScale` would
  scale it a second time.
- **`ZIndex = 10 + slot`** on the buttons, belt-and-braces only: the rects are disjoint by
  construction (proved in `projection.spec`), so the ZIndex rule must never be load-bearing. Say so.
- **The selected card's rect is unchanged by the lift**, because the rect already spans rest-to-lifted
  by construction, so the second tap lands where the first did (D-01).
- **At hand 4 the buttons stay `Active`** and still call `tapSlot`; the **reducer** makes the tap
  inert (F6). Hidden or inactive buttons would let a tap fall through to the backdrop and clear a
  selection.
- **A returning card is tappable for the whole flight** — the button is at the slot from the first
  frame; the flight is cosmetic and gates nothing (D-02, D-11).
- **Arrival order, never re-sorted by rank** (T-4-131): a re-sort on draw moves slots under a thumb.
- **The renderer diffs nothing for itself** (T-4-130): `apply` takes the authoritative `Display` **and**
  the `Change` list; cards are matched by `cardId`; a change naming a card this hand does not have is
