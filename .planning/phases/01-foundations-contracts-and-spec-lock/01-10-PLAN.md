   `require("...")` targets.
4. Re-resolve each target **by Roblox rules over the DataModel**: `./` from the script's parent,
   `../` from the parent's parent, `@self/` from the script itself, each further component a child,
   `..` a parent. Assert the target instance exists and is a `ModuleScript`.
