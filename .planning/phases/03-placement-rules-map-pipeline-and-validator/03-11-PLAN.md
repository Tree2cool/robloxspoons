evidence is complete and automated; the decision is the owner's.

reviews do not belong in the same plan.

but it is a Creator-Hub setting the repository cannot read, in the same class as `Players.MaxPlayers`
(M-05) and the FOUND-07 persistence toggle. It needs one reading in Studio.

Output: the owner's verdict appended to `03-G2-REPORT.md`, and `M-08` resolved one way or the other.
