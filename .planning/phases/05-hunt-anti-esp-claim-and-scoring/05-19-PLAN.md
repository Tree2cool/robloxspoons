<objective>
The phase gate. Three things, none of them code: the mutation ledger, the honest requirement split,
and the owner checkpoint.

Purpose: this phase's whole discipline rests on claims that **mutations were run**. Eighty-plus
mutations were specified across eighteen plans, and the orchestrator was explicit about why that
matters here: *"three gates in this repo were written, looked right, and could not fire. One recent
example: a check for `player.Character` silently matched `player.CharacterAdded`, so the mutant
passed."* A machine cannot assert that a human ran a mutation. **So the ledger is the artifact that
makes the claim auditable**, and a mutation with no recorded observed count is treated as not run.

The second job is honesty about what is proved. Two of Phase 5's five ROADMAP criteria are not
machine-checkable at all — the client-DataModel replication audit and the Gate G3 session — and three
whole files (`HuntService`, `HuntClient`, `SpoonView`) cannot be loaded by Lune and so have **no
behavioural machine coverage whatsoever**, only source scans. Writing "HUNT-01 complete" would be a
false claim about the game.

The third job is to put three decisions in front of the owner that were taken without them, in the
order they matter.

Output: `05-MUTATION-LEDGER.md`, `05-VALIDATION-RESULTS.md`, updated `REQUIREMENTS.md` rows, and a
blocking checkpoint.

**This plan writes the RESULTS, not the strategy.** The validation **strategy** —
`05-VALIDATION.md` — was committed alongside the plans, **before** execution, as Phase 4 did
(Nyquist 8e). It is the contract: the test infrastructure, the sampling rate, the per-requirement
test map, the wave-0 requirements, the mandatory per-gate mutation shape, and the manual-only list.
This plan's job is to fill in what actually happened and to state where the two diverge. **If a
requirement's real evidence differs from what `05-VALIDATION.md` planned, that divergence is a
finding to record, not a document to quietly rewrite.**
