**Three decisions this plan takes:**

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Nine functions, not one.** Each returns its own problems list and its own non-vacuity size | Research Pattern 6 says "one problems-function each so a failure names itself". A single 200-line checker that reports "HuntService has 1 problem" costs the debugging session the named one saves. Nine also means nine independent non-vacuity sizes, so a slice that silently returned `""` is caught |
| 2 | Every needle gets a comment of the form `-- needle "<X>": the mutant replaces it with "<Y>", which does not contain "<X>"` | This is the `player.Character` lesson turned into a habit. Writing the sentence forces the author to look at the mutant text, which is the step that was skipped the three times a gate in this repo could not fire |
| 3 | Every slice is **two-anchor** (`slice(text, from, to)`), never a single `find` | `HuntService`'s closures are short and adjacent. A single-anchor slice that overruns into the next closure makes the scan check the wrong code and pass for the wrong reason — the `round_end.spec` `ENDER`/`VIEW_HEAD` lesson |
