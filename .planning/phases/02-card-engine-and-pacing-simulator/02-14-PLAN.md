<objective>
Run the sweep plan 02-10 built, triage anything that ended on the action cap, and commit the report
and the machine-readable data together.

Purpose: this is the evidence Gate G1 is decided against. It is a separate plan from the tool because
the run is tens of minutes: entangling a forty-minute wait with writing the CLI means a bug surfaces
after the wait rather than in the smoke test, and it means the executor is holding a half-written
tool in context while polling a background job. Plan 02-11 then makes the decision against what this
plan commits, so the decision is a reviewable act rather than a side effect of a long run.
Output: `02-G1-REPORT.md` and `02-sweep-data.json`, committed together.

      is plan 02-13's own choice held constant for comparability and not something amendment A7 or
      its correction specifies.
   3. the CHOICE rule — plan 02-13 picks uniformly from the drawing seat's naturals. The reference
      prototype did the same, with exactly the same caveat.
   Report which of the three you ruled out and how, rather than naming one and stopping.
   Check `valveSeenResetsTotal` on the same cells while you are there: a non-zero count means
   `VALVE_SEEN_CAP` cleared the window and the valve was BLIND for part of those rounds, which

   the report has to call both "the valve did not help", and that is the one sentence in this whole
   phase that most needs to be precise.

   **Why this matters more than its cost suggests.** The one measured valve-on residual — 0.025% at
   k=2,j=2, one round in 4,000 — was observed under a 20,000-action cap, and nobody knows whether the
   round was livelocked or merely unfinished. If every candidate triages to `CapArtifact`, the report
   may say *zero observed stalls at the confirmed round count*. If any triages to
   `ResidualLivelock`, the report must say *zero stalls for the selected pool*, and say that the valve
   is a demonstrated mitigation rather than a guarantee. **Either way the report must claim only what
   was measured, and must not assert an unconditional guarantee or describe zero-stall as a property
   of the engine itself.** Amendment A7's own wording is stronger than the measurement supports. The
